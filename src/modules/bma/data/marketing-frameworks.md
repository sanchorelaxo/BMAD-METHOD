# Marketing Frameworks Reference

## Copywriting Frameworks

### AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
- **Attention**: Grab attention with a compelling headline
- **Interest**: Build interest with relevant information
- **Desire**: Create desire by highlighting benefits
- **Action**: Drive action with a clear CTA

### PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution)
- **Problem**: Identify the reader's problem
- **Agitate**: Amplify the pain of the problem
- **Solution**: Present your solution

### BAB (Before, After, Bridge)
- **Before**: Describe the current painful state
- **After**: Paint the picture of the desired state
- **Bridge**: Show how your solution gets them there

### 4 Ps (Promise, Picture, Proof, Push)
- **Promise**: Make a bold promise
- **Picture**: Help them visualize the outcome
- **Proof**: Provide evidence and social proof
- **Push**: Encourage immediate action

## Content Strategy Frameworks

### Hero, Hub, Help
- **Hero**: Big, tent-pole content pieces
- **Hub**: Regular, serialized content
- **Help**: Always-on, search-optimized content

### COPE (Create Once, Publish Everywhere)
- Create comprehensive content
- Adapt for each channel
- Maintain consistent messaging
- Maximize content ROI

## Email Marketing Frameworks

### Welcome Sequence Structure
1. Welcome + immediate value
2. Brand story + mission
3. Social proof + results
4. Overcome objections
5. Soft offer + CTA

### Cart Abandonment Sequence
1. Reminder (1 hour)
2. Urgency (24 hours)
3. Incentive (48 hours)
4. Final chance (72 hours)

## Advertising Frameworks

### Facebook Ad Structure
- **Hook**: First 3 seconds
- **Problem**: Identify pain point
- **Solution**: Present your offer
- **Proof**: Show results
- **CTA**: Clear next step

### Google Ads Best Practices
- Include keyword in headline
- Highlight unique value
- Use numbers and specifics
- Include strong CTA
- Leverage ad extensions

## SEO Frameworks

### E-A-T (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- Demonstrate subject expertise
- Build authoritative backlinks
- Establish trust signals

### Keyword Research Process
1. Seed keyword brainstorm
2. Competitor analysis
3. Search intent mapping
4. Difficulty assessment
5. Content gap identification

## Analytics Frameworks

### AARRR (Pirate Metrics)
- **Acquisition**: How users find you
- **Activation**: First value experience
- **Retention**: Users coming back
- **Revenue**: Monetization
- **Referral**: Users bringing others

### North Star Metric
- Single metric that best captures value
- Aligns team around growth
- Leading indicator of success
