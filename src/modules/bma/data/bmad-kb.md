# BMad Marketing & Advertising Knowledge Base

## Overview

BMad Marketing & Advertising Extension adapts the BMad-Method framework for marketing campaigns, advertising, and growth initiatives. This extension provides specialized agents, workflows, and tools designed specifically for marketers and advertisers.

### Key Features

- **Specialized Marketing Agents**: Content writers, paid ads specialists, SEO experts, and more
- **Complete Marketing Workflows**: From strategy to execution and optimization
- **Channel-Specific Support**: Tailored checklists and templates for each marketing channel
- **Data-Driven Approach**: Analytics and automation integration
- **Interactive Development**: Elicitation-driven campaign planning

### When to Use BMad Marketing & Advertising

- **Content Marketing**: Blog posts, articles, SEO content
- **Email Marketing**: Campaigns, sequences, automation
- **Social Media**: Organic and paid social strategies
- **Paid Advertising**: PPC, display, social ads
- **SEO**: Technical, on-page, and off-page optimization
- **E-commerce**: Product marketing, conversion optimization

## How BMad Marketing & Advertising Works

### The Core Method

BMad Marketing & Advertising transforms you into a "Marketing Director" - orchestrating specialized AI agents through the marketing process:

1. **You Strategize, AI Executes**: You provide marketing vision; agents handle execution details
2. **Specialized Agents**: Each agent masters one marketing discipline
3. **Structured Workflows**: Proven marketing patterns guide your campaigns
4. **Iterative Optimization**: Continuous testing and improvement

### The Three-Phase Approach

#### Phase 1: Strategy & Planning

- Define campaign objectives and KPIs
- Identify target audiences and personas
- Develop messaging and positioning
- Create content calendars and campaign briefs

#### Phase 2: Creation & Execution

- Generate content across channels
- Build email sequences and automation
- Create ad copy and creative briefs
- Optimize for SEO and conversion

#### Phase 3: Analysis & Optimization

- Track performance metrics
- Analyze campaign results
- A/B test and iterate
- Scale successful campaigns
