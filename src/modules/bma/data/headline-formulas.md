# Headline Formulas Reference

## Number Headlines
- "7 Ways to [Achieve Desired Outcome]"
- "10 [Things] Every [Audience] Should Know"
- "5 Mistakes [Audience] Make (And How to Avoid Them)"
- "21 [Resources] to [Achieve Goal]"

## How-To Headlines
- "How to [Achieve Goal] in [Timeframe]"
- "How to [Achieve Goal] Without [Pain Point]"
- "How to [Achieve Goal] (Even If [Obstacle])"

## Question Headlines
- "Are You Making These [Topic] Mistakes?"
- "What's the Best Way to [Achieve Goal]?"
- "Why Do [Audience] Struggle With [Problem]?"
- "Want to [Achieve Goal]? Here's How"

- "What [Experts] Don't Tell You About [Topic]"

- "[Achieve Goal] in Just [Timeframe]"

- "How [Person/Company] [Achieved Result]"

- "[Expert] Reveals [Secret/Strategy]"
- "The [Strategy] That Helped [Person] [Achieve Result]"
