# Content Frameworks Reference

## Blog Post Frameworks

### The Skyscraper Technique
1. Find popular content in your niche
2. Create something significantly better
3. Reach out to those who linked to the original
4. Promote your superior version

### The Hub and Spoke Model
- **Hub**: Comprehensive pillar content
- **Spokes**: Related supporting articles
- Internal linking connects all pieces
- Builds topical authority

### The Content Upgrade Strategy
1. Create valuable blog post
2. Offer bonus content (checklist, template)
3. Gate behind email opt-in
4. Build email list while providing value

## SEO Content Guidelines

### Content Length by Intent
- **Informational**: 1,500-3,000 words
- **Commercial**: 1,000-2,000 words
- **Transactional**: 500-1,000 words
- **Navigational**: As needed

### Keyword Placement
- Title tag (front-loaded)
- H1 heading
- First 100 words
- Subheadings (naturally)
- Image alt text
- Meta description
